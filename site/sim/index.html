      Same controller as the Python study, ported to JavaScript, with MuJoCo 3.17 compiled to WebAssembly. With sensor noise off, 40 of 40 test throws gave the same outcome, plan and catch times as the Python version; with noise, 86.5% of 200 random throws were caught (Python: 85% of 300). The robot's visual meshes are simplified for the web; its collision shapes and physics are unchanged.
